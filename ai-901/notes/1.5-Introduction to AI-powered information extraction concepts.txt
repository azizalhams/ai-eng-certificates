Information extraction is a workload that combines multiple AI techniques to extract data from content. 

 

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is a technology that automatically converts visual text in images into editable, searchable text data. The stages in the OCR process are: 

Image acquisition and input. 

Preprocessing and image enhancement. 

Text region detection. 

Character recognition and classification. 

Output generation and post-processing. 

 

Field extraction is the process of taking text output from OCR and mapping individual text values it to specific, labelled data fields that correspond to meaningful business information. The stages in the field extraction process are: 

OCR output ingestion. 

Field detection and candidate identification. 

Field mapping and association. 

Data normalization and standardization. 

Integration with business processes and systems. 

 

1. Which of these statements accurately defines AI information extraction? 

	Analyzing unstructured content to identify and extract relevant fields and values. 

2. How is OCR used in the information extraction process? 

	Optical character recognition (OCR) is used to convert images of text into machine-readable text data. 

3. How does generative AI enhance the data extraction process? 

	By using semantic language models to accurately match extracted values to data fields.
